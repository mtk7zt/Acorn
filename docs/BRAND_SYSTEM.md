# Acorn Brand & Visual Design System
## Living Cinematic Intelligence

### 1. Brand Essence & Positioning
- **Product Name:** Acorn / Acorn AI
- **Category:** Personal Career Intelligence (powered by Acorn Engine v1: AI Autonomous Job Tracker Engine)
- **Primary Brand Promise:** *“Be first to what matters.”*
- **Supporting Positioning:** *“Acorn keeps watch across Canada's tech market, finds the opportunities worth your attention, and brings them to you while there's still time to move.”*
- **Core Principle:** A job board gives users inventory. Acorn gives users judgment.

### 2. Voice & Editorial Tone
Acorn's voice is **short, observant, calm, warm, confident, specific, and occasionally witty**.

**Signature Phrases:**
- “I kept watch overnight.”
- “I found one worth looking at.”
- “This lines up with the AWS work you're targeting.”
- “This one can wait.”
- “You're early.”
- “Want me to check your fit?”
- “I'll keep watch.”

**Anti-Patterns (Never Use):**
- Technical pipeline badges in customer UI (`Canada Verified`, `Duplicate Check Passed`, `SHA-256`, `Tier A Velocity Rank`)
- Overclaiming or hype (`revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `unlock your potential`, `100% guaranteed`)
- Fabricated metrics or invented applicant counts

### 3. Color System (Biological Futurism & Forest Intelligence)
Luxury does not mean monochrome. Acorn pairs deep soil and warm bark contrast with living botanical and cognitive accents:

| Token | Hex | Semantic Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `--soil-950` | `#0B0A08` | Primary deep nocturnal canvas & high-contrast grounding |
| `--bark-900` | `#21170F` | Warm organic surface depth, elevated panels, mascot cap shadow |
| `--acorn-600` | `#A85F2D` | Core brand copper/acorn shell & warm structural accents |
| `--acorn-400` | `#E39A58` | Warm amber-copper highlight, interactive warmth, seed body |
| `--cream-50` | `#FFF7EA` | Editorial printed card surface & primary warm daylight typography |
| `--sprout-500` | `#72D65C` | Living botanical sprout & active Acorn presence indicator |
| `--signal-300` | `#D9FF7A` | High-attention luminous signal lime (timely opportunity cue) |
| `--sky-400` | `#6EC8FF` | Contextual clarity, location/timing metadata, Fit Check scan |
| `--violet-400` | `#B89CFF` | Cognitive synthesis, pattern recognition, `thinking` state |
| `--coral-400` | `#FF8E7D` | Human warmth, encouragement, and constructive gap callouts |

`--sprout-500` indicates life and active Acorn state—never a “Canada Verified” badge, as Canadian market eligibility is an intrinsic baseline guarantee of the product.

### 4. Typography
- **Editorial & Emotional Statements:** `Instrument Serif` (Regular & Italic), used for hero statements, section headlines, and morning briefing greetings.
- **Product UI, Reasoning & Controls:** `Barlow` (weights `300`, `400`, `500`, `600`), used for Signal cards, Fit Check analysis, navigation, and natural-language controls.

### 5. Material & Surface Hierarchy
1. **Selective Liquid Glass (`LiquidGlass`):** Reserved for floating navigation, active communication portals, and primary interactive overlays.
2. **Warm Bark Panels (`--bark-900` / `#140F0A`):** Tactile, matte-bordered containers for overnight briefings and activity timelines.
3. **Printed Editorial Cream Cards (`--cream-50`):** High-legibility warm paper surfaces used for highlighted Morning Signals and resume preparation sheets.
